**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Kinden Corporation, together with its subsidiaries, provides integrated electrical and facility engineering services in Japan. It offers value-added electrical equipment within the enclosure including building, factories and commercial and public facilities; developing and maintaining electrical infrastructure such as overhead and/or underground transmission lines, distribution lines from and between power station and substation; air conditioning and sanitary system; air conditioning and sanitation systems equipment diagnosis; electrical systems equipment diagnosis; integrated building systems monitoring - building automation and control system; instrumentation systems for plants and other facilities; and field instrumentation services. The company also provides campus information communication equipment; wide area information communication equipment; living space improvements; industrial space consolidation; creates working spaces; supporting wide-ranging tasks from measurement and design to construction and maintenance; wind turbine generation systems; wind turbine generation system maintenance; and photovoltaic power generation optimization systems. constructs environment-friendly systems. Kinden Corporation was incorporated in 1944 and is headquartered in Osaka, Japan.
