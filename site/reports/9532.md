**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Osaka Gas Co., Ltd. provides natural gas, electricity, and other energy products and services in Japan and internationally. The company operates through three segments: Domestic Energy, Overseas Energy, and Life & Business Solutions. The Domestic Energy Business segment manufactures, supplies, and sells city gas; sells gas appliances; installs gas pipelines; sells and transports LNG; sells LPG and industrial gas; and generates and sells electricity, as well as engineering and construction for gas and environmental facilities. The Overseas Energy Business segment engages in development and investment related to natural gas; and supply of energy. The Life & Business Solutions Business segment is involved in real estate development and rental; information processing services; and the sale of fine materials and carbon material products. In addition, it is involved in home renovation; the sales of heat supply; engineering; operation, management, and maintenance of buildings and facilities; software development; manufacturing and selling fine materials, carbon material products, activated carbon, and wood protective coating. Osaka Gas Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1897 and is headquartered in Osaka, Japan.
