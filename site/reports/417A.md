**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Blue Zones Holdings Co.,Ltd. operates supermarkets in Japan. The company offers various products, including fresh food, dried and processed food, and daily food. It also engages in trading business and related operations; store-related operations, such as security, facility management, and insurance agency; import and wholesale of food products, beverages, alcohol, and raw materials; and assisting with food manufacturing, processing, and packaging, as well as other related tasks. The company was formerly known as Yaoko Co.,Ltd. and changed its name to Blue Zones Holdings Co.,Ltd. in October 2025. The company was founded in 1890 and is headquartered in Kawagoe, Japan.
