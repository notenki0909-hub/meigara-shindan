**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

The Chiba Bank, Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, provides banking products and services in Japan and internationally. It offers time, installment time, and foreign currency deposits; loan products, such as home, card, car, education, home renovation, personal, and other loans; credit and debit cards; and asset management products, including investment trusts, NISA, Walthnavi, public debt, defined contribution pension plan, and other asset management products. The company also provides insurance products comprising pension insurance, whole life insurance, medical insurance and cancer insurance, long-term care and dementia insurance, disability insurance, term life insurance, and endowment insurance; and testamentary trust and estate settlement services. In addition, it offers corporate products and services comprising fundraising business portal, Web-EB service, cashless payment, accounting and collection rationalization service, business and management support, and business succession support; internet services, foreign exchange services, and safe deposit boxes. Further, the company holds securities consisting of bonds, stocks, and investment trusts for investment and operating purposes. The Chiba Bank, Ltd. was incorporated in 1943 and is based in Chiba, Japan.
