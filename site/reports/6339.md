**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Sintokogio,Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, manufactures and sells foundry, surface treatment, casting, environmental, conveying, special machinery, and other equipment in Japan, China, Asia, the United States, Europe, and internationally. The company engages in electronic core materials and ceramics businesses. It also offers green sand and v-process molding, chemically bonded sand, and aluminium casting products; dust collection and fire prevention system, exhaust gas and water treatment products; powder treatment products and technologies; and force sensors. In addition, it provides flooring and electric cylinders, as well as image processing measuring machines and calibration of measuring instruments and machines. Further, it provides shot and air blasting machines, shot peening machines, barrel and precision brush polishing equipment, high-precision micro-machining equipment, surface evaluation equipment, surface treatment parts, abrasives, polishing, materials, etc.; mold molding and V process equipment, core molding and casting sand treatment equipment, and automatic pouring and sand coating equipment; casting equipment and parts, powder and granular processing equipment, wear-resistant castings, etc; dust collectors, deodorizers, wastewater treatment equipment, VOC gas purification equipment, and environmental business components; lifting devices, step-climbing machines; and organic EL panel manufacturing equipment and automated lines. Additionally, it provides dispersizers, battery raw material supply equipment, 3D equipment, 3D printers, ceramic products, molding equipment, automotive door assembly equipment, metal magnetic powders, sterile environment provision equipment; and special machinery business parts, nursing care and welfare products, etc. The company was formerly known as Kubota Seisakusho, Ltd. and changed its name to Sintokogio,Ltd. in February 1960. Sintokogio,Ltd. was incorporated in 1934 and is headquartered in Nagoya, Japan.
