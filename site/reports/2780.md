**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Komehyo Holdings Co.,Ltd. engages in the purchase, procurement, sale, brokerage, and auction of used and new products through stores in Japan, rest of Asia, the United States of America, Europe, and internationally. The company operates through Brand and Fashion Business, Tire and Wheel Business, and Real Estate Rental Business segments. The company offers jewelry, precious metals, watches, bags, fashion goods, clothing, kimonos, cameras, and musical instruments, as well as apparel and sneakers, and gemstones. It is involved in the brand fashion business, purchase and sale of used and new passenger car tires and wheels, and real estate leasing business, as well as maintenance, management, and cleaning of buildings and other structures. Komehyo Holdings Co.,Ltd. was founded in 1947 and is headquartered in Nagoya, Japan.
