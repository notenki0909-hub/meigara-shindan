**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Kawai Musical Instruments Manufacturing Co., Ltd. manufactures and sells musical instruments in China, Japan, North America, Europe, and internationally. It operates through Musical Instrument Education; Material Processing; and Others segments. It offers grand, upright, digital, and hybrid pianos; acoustics; and accessories. The company also operates music and gymnastics schools; engages in publishing business in the field of music and art; and learning centers. In addition, it develops and sells computer music software, as well as software for small businesses; manufactures and sells precision metals, interior wooden panels products, and electronic devices; provides soundproof rooms; provides physical education equipment; and promotes music appreciation through lectures and concerts. Further, it engages in information and financial related business; and operates as insurance agency. The company was founded in 1927 and is headquartered in Hamamatsu, Japan.
