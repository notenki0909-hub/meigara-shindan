**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Innotech Corporation, together with its subsidiaries, engages in the import and sale of electronic design automation software, electric components, and semiconductor products in Japan, China, and internationally. The company also develops and sells testers and CPU boards. It provides industrial boards/embedded systems, environmental boards/systems, EDA and OrCAD software, servers/storage solutions, noise analysis solutions, model-based development solutions, digital media analysis and inspection solutions, testers/probe cards, face recognition AI solution, and robot automation services. In addition, it is involved in the sale of quality verification software for media workflows; noise analysis; and model-based development consulting services. The company was incorporated in 1976 and is headquartered in Yokohama, Japan.
