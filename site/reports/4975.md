**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

JCU Corporation engages in the produces and sells chemicals, machines, and auxiliary equipment in Japan. It operates through Chemicals and Equipment business segments. The company offers surface treatment chemicals for the decoration and rustproofing of automobile parts, faucet parts, construction materials, etc.; and for use in the manufacturing processes of printed circuit boards (PCBs), electronic components and semiconductors, etc. used in electronic equipment. It provides full automatic plating and processing machines; etching and washing devices for PCBs; and surface treatment equipment, as well as offers electricity from solar power generation equipment. The company was formerly known as Ebara-Udylite Co., Ltd. and changed its name to JCU Corporation in October 2012. JCU Corporation was founded in 1957 and is headquartered in Tokyo, Japan.
