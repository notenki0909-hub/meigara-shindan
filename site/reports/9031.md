Nishi-Nippon Railroad Co., Ltd. engages in the transportation business in Japan and internationally. It operates through five segments: Transportation; Real Estate; Distribution; Logistics; and Leisure and Services. The company is involved in the operation of railway, taxi, and bus business; real estate rental and sale business, and overseas development projects; operate road construction and bus services; involved in rental and housing businesses; operation of supermarkets and restaurants business; engages in hotel operations, travel agencies, and advertising businesses; international and domestic logistics business; developing agricultural business projects, as well as operates hotels, travel, entertainment, and food and sub contracted catering businesses; engages in the IC card, maintenance and construction related, metal recycling, and agricultural related business; and operates amusement park and the aquarium. Nishi-Nippon Railroad Co., Ltd. was founded in 1908 and is headquartered in Fukuoka City, Japan.
