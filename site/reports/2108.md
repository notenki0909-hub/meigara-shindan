**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Nippon Beet Sugar Manufacturing Co.,Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the manufacture, sale, logistics, and distribution of food products in Japan. It operates through Sugar, Food, Feed, Agricultural Materials, Real Estate, and Other segments. The company offers beet, refined, and pocket sugar; beet and refined molasses; baker's yeast, oligosaccharides, betaine, and other functional food; compound and functional feed for dairy and beef cattle; beet pulp; seeds; and technical assistance solutions, including feed component analysis and design, and farming support services. It also manufactures and sells paper tubes, seedling containers and pots, soil packing and seeding tools, and beet transplanting equipment; and agricultural machinery and equipment. In addition, the company is involved in the transportation and storage of beet sugar raw materials and finished beet sugar products; transportation of compound feed; construction and leasing of commercial facilities, including office buildings, commercial spaces, and other properties; sale of coal, petroleum products, and automotive parts; operation of insurance agencies and sports facilities; and research and development activities under food, agricultural equipment and materials, sugar beets, and livestock areas. It sells its sugar and food products through its sales agents and its online shop. Nippon Beet Sugar Manufacturing Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1919 and is headquartered in Chuo, Japan.
