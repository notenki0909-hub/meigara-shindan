**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Saibu Gas Holdings Co.,Ltd. produces, supplies, and sells natural gas in Japan. It operates through four segments: Gas, LPG, Electricity and Other Energy, and Real Estate. The Gas segment includes the production, supply, and sale of city gas, sales of gas appliances, gas piping construction, LNG sales, and renovation services. The LPG segment covers the sale of LPG, LPG equipment, and related construction work. The Electricity and Other Energy segment comprises electricity sales, heat supply, and solar power generation, as well as international energy business such as overseas LNG shipments. The Real Estate segment involves the sale, leasing, and management of real estate, including residential construction and land development. The company also provides information processing services, investment activities, restaurant operations, and manufactures and sells carbon materials, paints, and chemical products. The company was formerly known as Saibu Gas Co.,Ltd. and changed its name to Saibu Gas Holdings Co.,Ltd. in April 2021. Saibu Gas Holdings Co.,Ltd. was founded in 1902 and is headquartered in Fukuoka City, Japan.
