**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Iriso Electronics Co., Ltd. develops, manufactures, develops, and sells multi-pin connectors for automotive (in-vehicle) equipment, digital equipment, and industrial equipment in Japan, the rest of Asia, Europe, and North America. The company offers board-to-board, FPC/FFC, wire harness, socket, pin header, input/output, and card edge connectors; compression terminals for cable connections, interface connectors, and signal connections; and electrostatic discharge protector chips. It also provides automation connectors comprising connectors for ADAS sensing devices, powertrain connectors, and infotainment connectors; connectors for home and commercial appliances; and connectors for industrial equipment. The company was incorporated in 1966 and is headquartered in Yokohama, Japan.
