**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

MegaChips Corporation, a semiconductor company, designs, develops, manufactures, and sells products centering on system LSIs in Japan and internationally. The company offers application-specific integrated circuits; application-specific standard products comprising semiconductors and tool kits; and modularized semiconductor products. It also develops analog IP platforms, security technologies, memory control technologies, and communication solutions such as Wi-Fi. MegaChips Corporation was incorporated in 1967 and is headquartered in Osaka, Japan.
