**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Daito Trust Construction Co.,Ltd., together with its subsidiaries, engages in construction, real estate leasing, real estate development, and other businesses in Japan. The company constructs apartments and condominiums; and planning, proposal, and construction of rental housing. It is also involved in the provision of brokerage and building management service; development and sale of residential and non-residential buildings, and overseas real estate. In addition, the company engages in land acquisition, development of investment-type condominiums, healthcare, and logistics facilities, as well as purchasing, renovation, and resale business; provision of construction loans; and care and nursery school, LP gas supply, and biomass power generation businesses. Daito Trust Construction Co.,Ltd. was incorporated in 1974 and is headquartered in Minato, Japan.
