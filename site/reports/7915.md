**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Nissha Co., Ltd. engages in industrial materials, devices, medical technologies, information and communication, and pharmaceutical and cosmetics businesses in Japan and internationally. The company offers decorative film-molded parts, such as in-mold decoration technology and electronics, transfer foil, molding, CMF design sample book products, commercial printing; and sustainable materials, including metallized paper and board, and sustainable molded products. It is involved in the provision of touch, force, and gas sensors; the design and manufacture of medical technologies; development and manufacture of pharmaceuticals and quasi-drugs; design, development and manufacturing service of products such as surgical instruments for minimally invasive treatments and medical wearable sensors; and manufacturing and distributing patient monitoring and medical surgical devices. In addition, the company offers publication, high-definition digital archive photography, and fabric printing products and services; contract development and manufacturing film types of pharmaceuticals, such as oral thin films and transdermal patches; development and manufacturing of over-the-counter (OTC) and prescription drugs under contract manufacturing (CDMO); and dissolving microneedle patches; as well as produces and sells business media, including industrial recording materials and ticket/card materials. It serves mobility, mobile devices, home appliance, medical, food and consumer goods, cosmetics, and industrial equipment markets. The company was formerly known as Nissha Printing Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Nissha Co., Ltd. in 2017. Nissha Co., Ltd. was founded in 1929 and is headquartered in Kyoto, Japan.
