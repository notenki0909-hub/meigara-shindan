**事業内容（English・出典 Yahoo Finance）**

Meito Co., Ltd. engages in the manufacture and sale of confectionery, beverages, seasoned foods, food additives, and other food products in Japan and internationally. It provides chocolates, such as alphabet, nut, best assortment, strawberry, adult-oriented liquor, crunchy dome, creamy, cookie crunch, peanut, donut, and acorn picnic chocolates; and premix such as lemon, apple, peach, royal milk, stickmate rooibos and jasmine, warm ginger, and melting fruit tea; lemonade, masala chai, stickmate assortment, aquamate, milk cocoa, and jelly. The company also offers candies, which includes soda mix, fruit drops, butter salt, crushed slated lemon, mineral salt candy; vitamin C, herb mint ice creams, milk, ginger, and manuka honey throat lozenges; chocolate balls, café au lait, chocolate-flavored shine muscat grapes, chocolate banana, melon soda, black sugar kinako stick, berele vanilla and strawberry multi, white berele, strawberry ice bar, grappestro and soda, and grape cafe au lait and melon; coffee, pudding and vanilla ice cream; chewy and bouncy fruit bar white peach, grape, and pineapple; and strawberry, uji kintoki, and coffee float. In addition, it provides nutritious food products, such as watermate apple and grape, hot mate honey lemon, kudzu jelly mix with iron and zinc, black sugar flavor, delicious and healthy throat lozenges blueberry flavor and lemon herb, delicious and healthy chocolate and strawberry flavor, delicious and healthy cocoa and café au lait. Further, the company manufactures and sells pharmaceuticals, quasi-drugs, medical devices, veterinary drugs, cosmetics, and other chemical products. Further, the company constructs and manages public golf courses; and rents real estate properties. The company was formerly known as Meito Sangyo Co., Ltd. and changed its name to Meito Co., Ltd. in September 2025. Meito Co., Ltd. was incorporated in 1945 and is headquartered in Nagoya, Japan.
